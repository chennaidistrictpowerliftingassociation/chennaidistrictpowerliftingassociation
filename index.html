About CDPA

Chennai District Powerlifting Association

Building Strength in Chennai

Chennai District Powerlifting Association (CDPA) is dedicated to developing, encouraging and supporting powerlifting athletes in Chennai District.

CDPA covers Equipped Powerlifting, Classic Powerlifting, Bench Press and Dead Lift, providing athletes a platform to participate, compete and progress.

Registration: Registered under the Tamil Nadu Societies Registration Act 1975 and Tamil Nadu Societies Registration Rules 1978. Reg. No. 552/2017.

Affiliation: Affiliated to Tamilnadu Powerlifting Association and Powerlifting India.

Rules & Regulations

Chennai District Powerlifting Association

IPF Technical Rules

The technical rules of powerlifting shall be followed as applicable to the competitions conducted by the Association.

View IPF Technical Rules Book 2024

CDPA Athlete Eligibility

Sub Junior: 01.01.2008 to 31.12.2014

Junior: 01.01.2003 to 31.12.2007

Senior: No Age Limit

Masters-1: 01.01.1977 to 31.12.1986

Masters-2: 01.01.1967 to 31.12.1976

Masters-3: 01.01.1957 to 31.12.1966

Masters-4: Born before 1956

The above age-category date ranges shall move forward by one year for every subsequent competition year.

Competition Administration

The Association reserves the right to modify the prize distribution rules and related competition provisions during District-level competitions, as may be notified by the Association through an official circular.
